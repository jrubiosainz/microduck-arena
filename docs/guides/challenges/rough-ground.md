# Rough Ground

**Planned — submissions are not open.**

The goal is to walk over uneven ground. The Arena’s terrain and scoring rules
are not defined yet.

To prepare, try `Mjlab-Velocity-Rough-MicroDuck`. Increase terrain difficulty
slowly and test on unfamiliar surfaces. This training environment is a starting
point, not a reproduction of a released Arena course.
