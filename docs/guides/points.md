# How points work

Points reward participation and progress across challenges. Your challenge result
and your player points are separate.

| Action | Points | Limit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| First publication in a challenge | +100 | Once per challenge |
| Publish a run | +10 | First 3 per UTC day, across all challenges |
| Improve your personal best | +25 | Once per challenge |
| Hold a podium place | +250 / +150 / +100 | First / second / third, per challenge |
| Reach a weekly cup's last four | +250 / +150 / +100 | Champion / finalist / each semi-finalist, per cup |
| Cast a vote | +2 | Within the challenge’s voting limit and active window |

Bonuses add up: your first publication earns **110 points** if your daily
publication bonus is still available. Private tests earn no points.

**Personal best** means a better measured result: a lower average time, or the
same time with more finishes; on a rated challenge, a higher rating. Receiving
votes does not trigger this bonus.

**Cup points** are paid when the cup's final has aired, and stay yours.

**Podium points move with your rank.** Dropping from first to second removes
100 points. You need at least one finish; vote-ranked entries also need an
active vote. Publication bonuses do not require a finish.

**Your total can decrease** when voting points expire, you lose a podium place,
or a run is withdrawn or no longer eligible under the current scoring version.
